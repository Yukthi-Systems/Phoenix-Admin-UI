### Step 2 of 3 — Mail Policies

![Step 2: Mail Policies.](../../images/mailbox-step2-policies.png)

_Step 2: Mail Policies._

Everything on this step is optional.

| Field               | What it means                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| General Policy      | A general mail-handling policy to apply to this mailbox.                       |
| Forwarding Policy   | Automatically forwards this mailbox's mail according to a chosen policy.       |
| Distribution Policy | Treats this mailbox as part of a distribution list, following a chosen policy. |

> **Note:** You can't select both Forwarding Policy and Distribution Policy. Picking a Forwarding Policy automatically locks Distribution Policy — to use Distribution Policy instead, clear the Forwarding Policy first.
