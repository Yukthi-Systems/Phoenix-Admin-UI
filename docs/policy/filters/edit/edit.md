### Edit Filter Policy

Click Edit on a policy to update it. Like Adding a New Filters Policy (section 8.2), this is a single page, not a multi-step wizard — it's the exact same screen as Add, just pre-filled with the policy's current values. Fill in your changes and click Update Filters Policy at the bottom.

The same fields as Adding a New Filters Policy — Domain (read-only), Policy Name, Active, Allowed List (White Entries) and Blocked List (Black Entries).

> **Note:** At least one entry is required, in either the Allowed or the Blocked list. An entry also can't conflict with itself — for example you can't allow a whole domain while blocking one specific email address on that same domain, or the other way around.
