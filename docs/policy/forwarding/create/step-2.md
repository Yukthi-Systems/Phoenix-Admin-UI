### Step 2 of 3 — Additional

![Step 2: Additional settings.](../../images/policy-forwarding-step2-additional.png)

_Step 2: Additional settings._

Everything on this step is optional — leave any of these empty and that part of the rule simply won't apply.

| Field                  | What it means                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Forward to emails      | Where matching mail gets forwarded to. Pick an existing mailbox from the dropdown or type an email address, then click Add. You can add more than one. |
| From emails            | Only forward mail coming from these sender addresses or domains. Leave empty to forward mail from any sender.                                          |
| Specific Email Subject | Only forward mail whose subject line matches an entry here. Leave empty to forward mail with any subject.                                              |
