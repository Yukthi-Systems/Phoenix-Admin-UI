### Step 2 of 3 — Blocking & Exceptions

![Step 2: Blocking & Exception Settings.](../../images/policy-step2-blocking.png)

*Step 2: Blocking & Exception Settings.*

This step sets which mail this policy blocks, split into four matching sections — Incoming Email, Outgoing Email, Incoming Domain and Outgoing Domain — plus one size limit:

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| General / Outgoing Emails Blocked | Turn this on to block all incoming (or outgoing) mail for this policy, except any addresses you list below it as exceptions. |
| Incoming / Outgoing Exception Emails | Specific email addresses that are let through even while the matching Blocked toggle above is on. Type an address and click Add. |
| Incoming / Outgoing Domains Blocked | The same idea as above, but blocking (or allowing) mail by whole domain instead of a single address. |
| Incoming / Outgoing Exception Domains | Specific domains that are let through even while the matching Domains Blocked toggle is on. Type a domain and click Add. |
| Outgoing Size Limit (MB) | The largest outgoing email this policy allows, in megabytes. |

> **Note:** Each of the four Blocked toggles works the same way and is independent of the others — you can turn on any combination of them, each with its own exception list.
