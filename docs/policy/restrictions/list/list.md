## 8.6 Restriction Policies

A restriction policy controls where mail access is allowed from — by country/region, or by IP address.

Where to find it: click Policies in the left menu, then Restrictions.

This page lists every restriction policy you've created, for the domain selected at the top right.

![The Restriction Policies list.](../images/policy-restriction-list.png)

_The Restriction Policies list._

| Field                   | What it means                                                    |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Policy Name             | Click it to open the policy's details.                           |
| Status                  | Active or turned off.                                            |
| Created At / Updated At | When the policy was first created, and when it was last changed. |

### The Row Actions Menu

Click the ⋮ (three dots) at the end of any policy's row:

![The row actions menu on a restriction policy.](../images/policy-restriction-row-menu.png)

_The row actions menu on a restriction policy._

| Field                     | What it means                                                               |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Edit Restriction Policy   | Change this policy's settings.                                              |
| Delete Restriction Policy | Permanently removes the policy. This cannot be undone, so use it carefully. |

> **Note:** Unlike the other policy types, restriction policies don't have a Copy option here.

### The Actions Menu (Bulk Tools)

Click the Actions button (top right) for ways to work with restriction policies in bulk — the same as General Policies, see section 8.1:
