## 3.4 Adding a New Department

Click Add Single Department (or + Add Department, if shown). You'll fill in 3 short steps, and can click Previous at any point to go back and fix something.

### Step 1 of 3 — Department Information

![Step 1: Department Information.](../../images/dept-step1-info.png)

_Step 1: Department Information._

| Field           | What it means                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Department Name | A clear, descriptive name for the department, e.g. "Sales" or "IT Support". This is required and is how the department will be identified everywhere else in the panel. |

> **Note:** Only letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens (-) and underscores (\_) are allowed in the Department Name — other special characters aren't.
