# 10. Sessions

The Sessions menu shows who's currently signed in, and from where — across mailbox access, the Mail 25 mobile app, and single sign-on. It has three screens: Mailbox, Mail 25 App and SSO.

Where to find it: click Sessions in the left menu, then the screen you want.

## 10.1 Mailbox

This screen shows active mailbox sessions for the domain selected at the top right. Pick a domain from the dropdown to see its mailbox sessions.
