### Step 3 of 4 — Contacts

![Step 3: Contacts.](../../images/org-step3-contacts.png)

_Step 3: Contacts._

A contact is a real person at this organization you can reach out to if something needs attention. At least one is required.

| Field | What it means                                                                                                      |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Name  | The contact person's full name.                                                                                    |
| Phone | Their phone number. Pick the correct country code from the dropdown first.                                         |
| Email | Their email address.                                                                                               |
| Type  | What this person handles, e.g. "Technical" or "Accounts" — just a label to help you remember who to call for what. |
| Notes | Anything else worth remembering about this contact.                                                                |

Click + Add Contact to save it, then click Review to move to the last step.
