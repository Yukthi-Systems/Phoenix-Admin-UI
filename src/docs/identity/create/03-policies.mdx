## Policies and access

### Restriction policy

Optional. Pick a ready-made policy from the dropdown to control where this
identity may access mail from — by country/region or IP address.

### Department

Optional. Pick the department this identity belongs to.

## Services and applications

Further down the step, turn each service on or off individually for this
identity.

### Mailbox enabled

Turns on email for this identity.

### Chat enabled

Turns on chat for this identity.

### Files enabled

Turns on file storage for this identity.

### Mailbox quota / files quota (GB)

How much storage this identity gets for mailbox and files. Each field appears
only when its matching service above is turned on.

<Callout type="tip">
  Quotas come out of your organization's total allocation, so what you can give
  here depends on how much is still free.
</Callout>
