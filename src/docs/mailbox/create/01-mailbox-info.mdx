## Mailbox info

### E-Mail identity

Pick the existing identity this mailbox belongs to.

<Callout
  type="warning"
  title="Only the selected domain's identities are listed"
>
  The dropdown shows identities belonging to the domain currently selected at
  the top right. If the identity you want is missing, check that you are on the
  right domain — and that the identity has actually been created.
</Callout>

### Allocate space (GB)

How much storage this mailbox gets. The minimum is **0.1 GB** (100 MB), and the
space comes out of your organization's remaining allocation.
