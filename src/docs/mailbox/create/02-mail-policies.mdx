## Mail policies

Everything on this step is optional — leave it all empty and the mailbox simply
follows the domain's own settings.

### General policy

A general mail-handling policy to apply to this mailbox.

### Forwarding policy

Automatically forwards this mailbox's mail according to the chosen policy.

### Distribution policy

Treats this mailbox as a group address, following the chosen policy.

<Callout
  type="warning"
  title="Forwarding and distribution are mutually exclusive"
>
  You cannot select both. Picking a forwarding policy locks the distribution
  policy field — clear the forwarding policy first if you want to use
  distribution instead.
</Callout>
