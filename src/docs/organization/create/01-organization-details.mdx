## Organization details

### Organization name

The company's name, as it will appear everywhere else in the panel.

<Callout type="warning">
  The name cannot be changed after the organization is created — check the
  spelling before you move on. If you do need to correct it later, use **Rename
  Organization** from the row actions menu on the organizations list.
</Callout>

Name fields throughout this wizard (Organization Name, Branch Name and so on)
accept letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens (`-`) and underscores (`_`) only.

### Allocated quota (GB)

The total storage space this organization may use, in GB, shared across mail,
chat and files. The note under the field tells you the maximum you are allowed
to allocate right now, based on what is still free in your own pool.

### Organization type

- **Customer** — a regular customer using your service.
- **Reseller** — they sell your service on to their own customers.
- **Partner** — another kind of business partner.

### Organization active

On by default, so the organization works as soon as it is created. Turn it off
if you want to set everything up now and switch it on later.

### Select parent organization

If this organization sits underneath a bigger one — for example, a customer of
one of your resellers — pick that parent here. Otherwise leave it as it is.

### Email identities allocation

How many identities this organization may create. Choose **Limited** and enter
a number (type it, drag the slider, or use the quick-set buttons), or choose
**Unlimited** for no cap.

<Callout type="tip" title="Identities, not mailboxes">
  An identity is the core account. A mailbox is added **on top of** an identity
  — it does not consume a separate identity from this allowance.
</Callout>

### Services configuration

Turn on whichever of **Email**, **Chat** and **File Service** this organization
should be able to use. Your choices are summarised again on the Preview step.

### Additional information

Optional: a short description, the organization's website, and its GST number.
