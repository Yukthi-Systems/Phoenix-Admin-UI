## Contacts

A contact is a real person at this organization you can reach if something
needs attention. At least one contact is required.

### Name

The contact person's full name.

### Phone

Their phone number — pick the correct country code from the dropdown first.

### Email

Their email address.

### Type

What this person handles, e.g. `Technical` or `Accounts`. It is just a label to
help you remember who to call for what.

### Notes

Anything else worth remembering about this contact.

<Callout type="tip">
  Click **+ Add Contact** to save each person to the list, then click
  **Review** to move to the final step.
</Callout>
