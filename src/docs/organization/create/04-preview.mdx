## Preview

Your last chance to check everything. Scroll down to see the organization's
details, its branches, its contacts, and which services (Email, Chat, File) are
turned on or off.

- Something wrong? Click **Previous** and fix it.
- Everything correct? Click **Create Organization** at the bottom to finish.

<Callout type="warning">
  Remember that the organization name cannot be changed after creation — this is
  the last point at which you can correct it with **Previous**.
</Callout>
