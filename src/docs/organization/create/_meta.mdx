# Add an organization

An organization is a company using your mail service — your own company, or
one of your customers. The **Add Organization** wizard has four steps:

1. **Organization Details** — name, quota, type, identity allowance and services.
2. **Branches** — the offices or locations that belong to the organization.
3. **Contacts** — the people you can reach out to at that organization.
4. **Preview** — check everything, then create.

<Callout type="info" title="Branches and contacts are mandatory">
  Every organization needs at least one branch and at least one contact. The
  wizard will not let you finish without them.
</Callout>

Open the **Guide** panel on any step to see help for just that step.
