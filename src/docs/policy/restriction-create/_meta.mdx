# Add a restriction policy

A restriction policy controls **where** mail access is allowed from — by
country or region, or by IP address. The wizard has three steps:

1. **Policy Information** — name, description and active state.
2. **Restrictions** — the geo-location and IP rules.
3. **Preview** — check everything, then create.

<Callout type="info">
  Apply the finished policy to an identity from that identity's **Policies**
  step. Unlike the other policy types, restriction policies have no **Copy**
  option in their row actions menu.
</Callout>

Open the **Guide** panel on any step to see help for just that step.
