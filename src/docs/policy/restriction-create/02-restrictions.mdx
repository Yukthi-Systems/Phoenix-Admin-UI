## Access restrictions

Both fields are optional and work independently — you can set one, both, or
neither.

### Geo location

Pick a country or region from the dropdown and click **Add** to restrict access
based on where the connection comes from. You can add more than one.

### IP address

Type an IP address, choose its subnet size from the dropdown next to it (e.g.
`24`), and click **Add** to restrict access by IP range. You can add more than
one.

<Callout type="warning">
  Restrictions apply to the identities this policy is attached to. Check the
  ranges carefully before applying it to someone who travels, or you will lock
  them out of their own mail.
</Callout>
