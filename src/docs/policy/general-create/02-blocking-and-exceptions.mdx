## Blocking and exceptions

This step is split into four matching sections — incoming email, outgoing
email, incoming domain and outgoing domain — plus one size limit.

### Emails blocked (incoming / outgoing)

Turn this on to block **all** incoming (or outgoing) mail under this policy,
except any addresses you list below it as exceptions.

### Exception emails (incoming / outgoing)

Specific email addresses that are let through even while the matching blocked
toggle above is on. Type an address and click **Add**.

### Domains blocked (incoming / outgoing)

The same idea, but blocking by whole domain instead of a single address.

### Exception domains (incoming / outgoing)

Specific domains let through even while the matching domains-blocked toggle is
on. Type a domain and click **Add**.

### Outgoing size limit (MB)

The largest outgoing email this policy allows, in megabytes.

<Callout type="info">
  The four blocked toggles are independent — you can turn on any combination of
  them, and each keeps its own exception list.
</Callout>
