# Add a general policy

A general policy blocks mail in bulk and then lets specific senders through as
exceptions. The wizard has three steps:

1. **Policy Information** — domain, name and active state.
2. **Blocking &amp; Exceptions** — what is blocked, what is allowed through, and the outgoing size limit.
3. **Preview** — check everything, then create.

<Callout type="info" title="Policies are created, then applied">
  Creating a policy does not change anything on its own. You apply it later —
  to a domain, a mailbox or an identity — from that record's own form.
</Callout>

Open the **Guide** panel on any step to see help for just that step.
