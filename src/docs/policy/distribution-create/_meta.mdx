# Add a distribution policy

A distribution policy turns a mailbox into a group address: mail sent to it is
meant for several people. It controls who is allowed to send to that mailbox,
and who its members are. The wizard has three steps:

1. **Policy Information** — domain, name, description and active state.
2. **Additional** — the send rule and the group's members.
3. **Preview** — check everything, then create.

<Callout type="info">
  Apply the finished policy to a mailbox from that mailbox's **Mail Policies**
  step. A mailbox cannot use a distribution policy and a forwarding policy at
  the same time.
</Callout>

Open the **Guide** panel on any step to see help for just that step.
