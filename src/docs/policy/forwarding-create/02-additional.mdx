## Additional settings

Everything on this step is optional — leave any field empty and that part of the
rule simply does not apply.

### Forward to emails

Where matching mail gets forwarded. Pick an existing mailbox from the dropdown
or type an email address, then click **Add**. You can add more than one.

### From emails

Only forward mail coming from these senders or domains. Leave empty to forward
mail from any sender.

### Specific email subject

Only forward mail whose subject line matches an entry here. Leave empty to
forward mail with any subject.

<Callout type="tip">
  The **from** and **subject** filters combine — leaving both empty forwards
  everything the mailbox receives, which is rarely what you want on a busy
  mailbox.
</Callout>
