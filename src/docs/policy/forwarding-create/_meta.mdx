# Add a forwarding policy

A forwarding policy automatically forwards mail to another address. You set who
it forwards to, and can narrow it to mail from specific senders or with a
specific subject. The wizard has three steps:

1. **Policy Information** — domain, name, description and active state.
2. **Additional** — where mail is forwarded, and the sender/subject filters.
3. **Preview** — check everything, then create.

<Callout type="info">
  Apply the finished policy to a mailbox from that mailbox's **Mail Policies**
  step. A mailbox cannot use a forwarding policy and a distribution policy at
  the same time.
</Callout>

Open the **Guide** panel on any step to see help for just that step.
