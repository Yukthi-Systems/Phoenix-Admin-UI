# Add a filters policy

A filters policy is a simple allow/block list. Instead of a blocking toggle with
exceptions — the way general policies work — you list exactly which addresses or
domains are allowed through and which are blocked.

Unlike the other policy types, this is a **single page**, not a multi-step
wizard. Fill it in and click **Create Filters Policy** at the bottom.
