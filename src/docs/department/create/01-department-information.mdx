## Department information

### Department name

A clear, descriptive name for the department, e.g. `Sales` or `IT Support`.
This is required, and it is how the department is identified everywhere else in
the panel.

<Callout type="info">
  Only letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens (`-`) and underscores (`_`) are allowed
  in the department name — other special characters are rejected.
</Callout>
