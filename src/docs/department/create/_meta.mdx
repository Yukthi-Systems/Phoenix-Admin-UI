# Add a department

A department groups people or mailboxes by team — Sales, HR, IT and so on — so
they are easier to organize and manage together. The wizard has three steps:

1. **Department Information** — the department's name.
2. **Department Details** — optional address, description, notes and authorized persons.
3. **Preview** — check everything, then create.

Open the **Guide** panel on any step to see help for just that step.
