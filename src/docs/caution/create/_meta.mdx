# Add a caution

A caution is a warning message shown to people before they act on something
risky — for example a banner on mail that came from outside your organization.
The wizard has three steps:

1. **Basic Information** — name, severity and description.
2. **Content** — the message itself, as text or HTML.
3. **Preview** — check everything, then create.

<Callout type="info">
  Once created, a caution can be attached to a domain from the domain's **Spam
  &amp; Templates** step.
</Callout>

Open the **Guide** panel on any step to see help for just that step.
