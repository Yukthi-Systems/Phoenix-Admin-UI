# Add a disclaimer

A disclaimer is a standard notice — a legal or confidentiality notice, for
example — that can be attached to outgoing mail. The wizard has three steps:

1. **Basic Information** — name and description.
2. **Content** — the notice itself, as text or HTML.
3. **Preview** — check everything, then create.

<Callout type="info">
  Once created, a disclaimer can be attached to a domain from the domain's
  **Spam &amp; Templates** step.
</Callout>

Open the **Guide** panel on any step to see help for just that step.
