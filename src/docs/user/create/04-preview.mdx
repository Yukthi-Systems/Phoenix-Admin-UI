## Preview

Check everything before you finish — scroll down to see the user's details and
the full list of permissions you granted.

- Something wrong? Click **Previous** and fix it.
- Everything correct? Click the button at the bottom to finish.

<Callout type="tip">
  Take a moment over the permissions list here. It is far easier to un-tick
  something now than to work out later why someone can reach a screen they
  should not.
</Callout>
